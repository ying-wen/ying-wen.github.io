# Ying Wen Open-Source Projects and Research Platforms

Canonical page: https://yingwen.io/en/projects/

## OpenR

Open-source framework for advanced LLM reasoning via process reward models, tree search, and reinforcement learning.

- Repository: https://github.com/openreasoner/openr
- Tags: LLM, Reasoning, RL

## SMARTS

Scalable multi-agent RL training school for autonomous driving. Powered NeurIPS 2022 driving challenge.

- Repository: https://github.com/huawei-noah/SMARTS
- Tags: Autonomous Driving, Simulation, MARL
- Recognition: 🏆 Best System Paper, CoRL 2020

## MALib

Parallel framework for population-based multi-agent RL. Supports self-play, league training, and PSRO.

- Repository: https://github.com/sjtu-marl/malib
- Tags: MARL, Framework, Population-based
- Recognition: JMLR 2023

## MAT (Multi-Agent Transformer)

Casting multi-agent RL as a sequence modeling problem. SOTA on cooperative benchmarks. Deployed in real-world multi-AGV warehouse systems.

- Repository: https://github.com/PKU-MARL/Multi-Agent-Transformer
- Tags: Transformer, MARL, Deployed
- Recognition: NeurIPS 2022

## ZSC-Eval

Evaluation toolkit and benchmark for multi-agent zero-shot coordination.

- Repository: https://github.com/sjtu-marl/ZSC-Eval
- Tags: Evaluation, Zero-Shot, MARL
- Recognition: NeurIPS 2024

## GEAR

GPU-centric experience replay system for large RL models. 6× throughput over DeepMind Reverb.

- Repository: https://github.com/bigrl-team/gear
- Tags: Systems, GPU, RL
- Recognition: ICML 2023

## Voice Memo Sync

Intelligently sync, transcribe, and organize Apple Voice Memos with AI. OpenClaw Skill.

- Repository: https://github.com/ying-wen/voice-memo-sync
- Tags: AI, Voice, Productivity
